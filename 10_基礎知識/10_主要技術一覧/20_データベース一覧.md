- [MySQL](https://www.mysql.com/jp/)
  - OSS, Oracle 製
- [SQLite](https://www.sqlite.org/)
  - OSS

  - Microsoft 製
- [MariaDB](https://mariadb.org/)

- [Oracle Database](https://www.oracle.com/jp/database/)
